How it works

From a blank page to a ready plan in minutes

  1. Tell us the basics

    Destination, starting city, travellers, days and your total budget.

  2. Choose how to travel

    Compare train, bus, cab, flight and more, ranked against your budget.

  3. Pick places, stay and food

    Add the sights you like, then a stay and meals that fit what is left.

  4. Get your itinerary

    A day-by-day plan with a cost breakdown, Google Map pins and booking links.

Everything in the plan

Built to be useful, not just pretty

💸

Cost breakdown you can trust

Travel, stay, food, local transport and activities are added up against your budget. Over budget? We tell you by how much, and what to change.

🗺️

Itinerary on a real Google Map

Your places become numbered pins by day, with the route between them and one-tap links to open each stop in Google Maps.

🚆

Every way to get there

Trains, buses, shared taxis, cabs, flights and self-drive compared for your route, with booking links to the official sites.

🛏️

Stays at every price

From dorm beds to premium hotels, picked to match what is left after travel.

🍛

Food that is local

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack ideas for each destination, with tourist-trap options marked.

📄

Take it with you

Copy your full plan as text and keep it on your phone. Payments are never taken here: you book on the official sites.

An honest note on prices. Fares, room rates and entry fees shown are indicative estimates, not live quotes. We link you to the official sites to confirm and book. No payments happen on Safar Planner. Right now the planner covers destinations in India.
